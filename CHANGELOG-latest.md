## Strata 0.3.0

**Made for bid folders**
- **Version stacking:** `Plans_rev1.pdf`, `Plans_rev2.pdf` and `Plans rev 3.pdf` collapse into one row that shows the newest, with a **"3 versions"** pill. Click the pill, or press **→**, to see the older versions underneath. It understands rev/R/v numbers, revision letters (Rev B), dates (`_2026-03-01`), `(2)` and `- Copy`. Sheet numbers like `A-101` are left alone. You can turn it off in Settings → Layout.
- **What changed (Ctrl+Shift+H):** every file in this folder and its subfolders that changed **since your last visit**, today, in the last 7 or 30 days, or since a date. Newest files come first. Esc goes back.
- **Space view (Ctrl+Shift+S, or the 4th view button):** a treemap of what's using space, colored by kind (drawings, PDFs and Office files, video, images, archives). Double-click a folder to look inside. On This PC it shows your drives. Every folder and drive also has "Show what's using space" on its right-click menu.

**Everyday speed**
- **Quick look carousel:** press **Space** for a big preview, then use ← → to flip through files. Enter opens the file; Space or Esc closes the preview.
- **Jump to folder (Ctrl+G):** a fuzzy search over the folders you use, ranked by how often and how recently you opened them. Type "smi bid" to reach `D:\Bids\2026\Smith Library`. Ctrl+Enter opens it in a new tab, Shift+Enter in the other pane. Grid view moved to **Ctrl+Shift+G**.
- **Focus filter (Ctrl+Shift+F):** show only PDFs, drawings, Office files, images, files changed this week, files with a given tag, or the same type as the selection. The filter stays on while you browse; Esc or ✕ turns it off.
- **Tabs that stick:** right-click a tab → **Lock tab to this folder**. Folders you open from a locked tab open in a new tab. Right-click a tab → **Tab color** to color it.

**Polish**
- **Smooth animations:** the active tab and the active pane's accent bar slide, the sidebar and preview slide in and out, and folders fade in. Choose None / Fast / Smooth in Settings → Layout; Strata also follows Windows' "Animation effects" setting.
- **Themed dialogs:** replace/skip conflicts, delete confirmations, update notes, uninstall and name prompts now use your theme instead of plain Windows boxes. The batch rename window is themed too.
- **Sounds and notifications:** when a copy or move that took longer than 10 seconds finishes, Strata plays a soft chime and shows a card with **Open folder**. If Strata isn't in front, you get a Windows notification instead. You can turn both off in Settings → File operations.
