## Strata 0.2.2

- **Rearrange the sidebar:** drag pinned folders, drives, tags and saved searches to reorder them, or drag a section header (Pinned, Drives, Tags, Saved searches, Recent) to move the whole section. A line shows where it will land. Right-click also has **Move up / Move down**, and right-clicking a header offers **Reset sidebar order**.
- The order, and which sections are collapsed, are remembered.
- Drive roots in **Recent** now show their name and icon ("Data3 (F:)") instead of just "F:".

Also in 0.2.1: the ghost minimize / maximize / close buttons behind Strata's title bar with Mica or Acrylic are gone.
