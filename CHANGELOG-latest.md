## Strata 0.2.1

- **Fixed for real:** the faint "ghost" minimize / maximize / close buttons behind Strata's own when the Mica or Acrylic background is on. Windows was drawing its own caption buttons into the see-through title bar; Strata now tells Windows not to draw them at all (the same fix Firefox uses). Snap layouts on the maximize button, Alt+Space and Alt+F4 keep working.
